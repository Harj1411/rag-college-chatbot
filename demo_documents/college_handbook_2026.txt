CAMPUSMIND UNIVERSITY — STUDENT HANDBOOK & ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2026

SECTION 1: ACADEMIC CALENDAR & EXAMINATION REGULATIONS
1.1 Semester Schedule:
- Autumn Semester commences on August 1st and concludes with end-semester examinations in the first two weeks of December.
- Spring Semester commences on January 10th and concludes with examinations in May.
- Mid-semester examinations carry 30% weightage, assignments and quizzes carry 20%, and final end-semester examinations carry 50% weightage.

1.2 Attendance Requirements:
- A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory in each registered course to be eligible to sit for the final semester examinations.
- Students with attendance between 65% and 74% due to verified medical emergencies may apply for condonation through the Dean of Student Affairs with valid medical certificates.
- Students with attendance below 65% will be awarded an 'F' (Fail/Detained) grade in the respective subject and must repeat the course during the supplementary semester.

1.3 Grading System:
- Grade O (Outstanding): 90 - 100 marks (Grade Point 10)
- Grade A+ (Excellent): 80 - 89 marks (Grade Point 9)
- Grade A (Very Good): 70 - 79 marks (Grade Point 8)
- Grade B+ (Good): 60 - 69 marks (Grade Point 7)
- Grade B (Above Average): 55 - 59 marks (Grade Point 6)
- Grade C (Average): 50 - 54 marks (Grade Point 5)
- Grade P (Pass): 40 - 49 marks (Grade Point 4)
- Grade F (Fail): Below 40 marks (Grade Point 0)

SECTION 2: FEE STRUCTURE AND SCHOLARSHIPS
2.1 Tuition and Hostel Fees:
- Annual Tuition Fee for B.Tech programs is INR 1,80,000 payable in two equal installments at the beginning of each semester.
- Hostel accommodation and mess fee is INR 45,000 per semester for double occupancy air-cooled rooms.
- Late fee payment penalty: A late fee of INR 100 per day is charged after the 10th working day of the semester start date.

2.2 Merit & Need-based Scholarships:
- Dean's Merit Scholarship: 50% tuition fee waiver for students maintaining a CGPA of 9.5 and above with no backlogs.
- Sports & Extracurricular Scholarship: Up to 35% tuition waiver for students representing the university at national or state level tournaments.
- Need-based Financial Aid: Available for families with annual income below INR 3.5 Lakhs upon submission of valid income certificates.

SECTION 3: PLACEMENT & INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES
3.1 Placement Eligibility:
- Students with a CGPA of 6.5 or above and no active standing backlogs are eligible to register with the Training & Placement Cell (TPC).
- Every registered student is entitled to receive up to 2 placement offers in the Dream/Super-Dream tier (CTC > 12 LPA).
- Mandatory summer internship of 8 weeks duration must be completed between the 6th and 7th semester for all undergraduate engineering students.

SECTION 4: CAMPUS CODE OF CONDUCT & HOSTEL RULES
4.1 Hostel Curfew:
- Campus gates close at 10:00 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends and university holidays.
- Night-out permissions must be applied through the digital campus portal at least 24 hours in advance with parent/guardian approval.

4.2 Zero Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy:
- Ragging in any form (physical, verbal, or mental) is strictly prohibited as per Supreme Court of India guidelines.
- Any student found guilty of ragging is subject to immediate suspension, rustication, and filing of a police FIR.
- Anti-ragging 24x7 Helpline: 1800-180-5522 / Email: antiragging@campusmind.edu

SECTION 5: LIBRARY AND DIGITAL RESOURCES
5.1 Central Library:
- Open Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM. During examination periods, the reading hall remains open 24x7.
- Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books simultaneously for a duration of 14 days, renewable once online.
